Fam-AI phone app

This folder has one file for the phone: Fam-AI.apk

How to install
1. Copy Fam-AI.apk onto an Android phone.
2. Open the file.
3. Allow install from this source if the phone asks.
4. Tap Install, then open Fam-AI.

The phone should be Android 8 or newer.

What the app does
Take a photo of a leaf. The app names a possible disease or pest and writes what to do today. It does not give a medicine dose. Take a spreading problem to the agriculture officer.

Full steps, including language and the field computer, are in START-HERE.md at the top of this project.
